## 2. THREE RULES THAT GOVERN THIS EDIT

1. **NO VOICEOVER.** The track has a sung vocal and the frames already carry lyric words. A third voice makes it noise. This is a text-only ad.
2. **ALL ad copy sits in the BOTTOM THIRD or on a full-bleed card.** The lyric box owns top-centre. Never run both in the same region.
3. **The music must hard-cut to silence at 0:06.** The track is dynamically flat — there is no drop, no build, no beat to land a punchline on. The silence *is* the hook mechanism. Do not soften it with a fade.

## 5. HOOK VARIANTS TO A/B TEST

Same body, only the opening changes.

| # | Treatment | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| **H1** *(default)* | Silent bait 0:00–0:06, slam at 0:06 | Warm audiences, retargeting, organic |
| **H2** | Slam moves to **0:02** — `This is your follow-up system.` drops over the empty room almost immediately, then the peace plays underneath the cost segment | Cold traffic — buy the hook earlier |
| **H3** | Open on source 0:13.5–0:15.0 (her turning) + `She booked with someone else at 11 AM.` then cut back to the peaceful open | Aggressive / high-competition placements |

Run H1 vs H2 first. Whichever holds 3-second retention above ~55% gets the budget.
